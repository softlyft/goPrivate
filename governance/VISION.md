# Vision

## Why goPrivate exists

Private conversation is often treated as a product feature bolted onto identity platforms. goPrivate exists to make **ephemeral communication** a first-class, open protocol — conversations that appear, end, and leave no durable server trail.

## Why ephemeral communication matters

Not every conversation should live forever in a cloud. Ephemerality reduces the blast radius of leaks, subpoenas, device loss, and casual browsing of old history. It also matches how many sensitive exchanges actually work: short-lived and intentional.

## Why the protocol matters more than the application

The official web client is a **reference client**. The official relay is a **reference relay**. Long-term success is independent implementations — other clients, languages, and hosting models — speaking the same protocol.

## Why self-hosting must remain possible

If privacy depends on trusting one company’s servers, it is fragile. Self-hosting keeps the trust boundary under the operator’s control and prevents the protocol from collapsing into a single SaaS product.

## Why the project is community-driven

Protocol evolution, security review, and reference code improve through public RFCs, ADRs, and contributions. Governance starts with a clear maintainer model and can grow into broader stewardship as the community matures.
