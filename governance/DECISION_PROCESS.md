# Decision process

## Current model: BDFL

goPrivate uses a **Benevolent Dictator For Life (BDFL)** model for major architectural decisions.

1. Ideas are discussed in Issues, Discussions, or RFCs.
2. Protocol changes should begin as an RFC in `rfcs/`.
3. Architectural choices that bind the project should be recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
4. The project maintainer makes the final call after community discussion.

## Evolution

As the community grows, governance may move toward a technical steering committee. That change will be documented here and in `MAINTAINERS.md` before it takes effect.

## Day-to-day

Maintainers merge routine fixes and documentation improvements under the expectations in `MAINTAINERS.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
